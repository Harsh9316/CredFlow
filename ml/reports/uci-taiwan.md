# Probability-of-default model: Default of credit card clients (Taiwan)

Generated by `python -m credrail_ml.train` on 2026-11-11. Advisory only: Credrail shows this next to the
policy decision and never uses it to approve or reject.

- **Data:** [Default of credit card clients (Taiwan)](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/350/default+of+credit+card+clients), CC BY 4.0. 24,000 training rows, 6,000 test rows (stratified 80/20 split, seed 42).
- **Excluded columns:** ID, SEX, MARRIAGE, AGE, EDUCATION.
- **Model:** logistic regression, L2, C = 0.03 chosen by 5-fold cross-validated AUC (0.7681 ± 0.0058).

## Limitations

- Trained on one lender's historical portfolio. Retrain and validate on your own book before relying on it.
- Probabilities describe the training population and drift as conditions change; monitor calibration.
- Not a substitute for the policy engine, credit judgement or regulatory model-risk review.
